It doesn't matter what your starting words are (mine are CRANE - TOILS - PUDGY, since that knocks out all the vowels and most common consonants in 3 guesses, if I don't get hits sooner). I used a scrabble dictionary combined with an a English word CSV that ranks any remaining 5 letter words by frequency of use in the English language.

It's not perfect, because NYT eliminate some valid words that end in `S`, `ED` and sometimes `Y`, so its not perfect because their dictionary is proprietary and mine can't Kep up with what they include and dont include (but I did try to handle some special cases to give warnings of possible non-solutions).
